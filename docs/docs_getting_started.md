# FACT Application - Getting Started Guide

Welcome to the **FACT Application**! 
FACT is a powerful tool built on Google Workspace to help our teams manage tasks, track projects, and streamline complex document approval workflows. 

Whether you are a new team member, an Executive, or a stakeholder checking on a request, this guide will help you get oriented quickly.

## 1. Accessing the App

Because FACT is built on Google Apps Script (GAS), accessing it is as simple as visiting a secure web link.

1. **Open the Web App Link:** Navigate to the FACT Web App URL provided by your administrator.
2. **Authentication:** You will be automatically authenticated using your `@gsa.gov` Google Workspace account. 
3. **Permissions:** Ensure you have been granted access. If you see an "Access Denied" message, please contact your administrator to be added to the appropriate access group (e.g., Admin, Executive, Guest).

> [!NOTE]
> If it's your first time running a Google Apps Script application, Google may ask you to authorize the application to run under your account. Follow the on-screen prompts to "Review Permissions" and "Allow" access.

---

## 2. The Main Dashboard (Navigating your data)

Once logged in, you will land on the Main Dashboard. The sidebar on the left allows you to switch between different data views depending on how you prefer to work.

FACT offers three primary ways to visualize your data:

### 📋 Grid View (Default)
The Grid view is the default dashboard view, presenting a tabular layout for detailed sorting.
- **Filtering & Sorting:** Click on column headers to sort, or use the search bar to filter for specific keywords, assignees, or projects.
- **Viewing Details:** To view or edit a task, click the link on the item row to open the detailed modal window.
- **Display Settings (Comfortable vs. Compact):** You can toggle the display density. The **Comfortable** setting provides more padding and whitespace for easier reading, while the **Compact** setting reduces padding to fit more rows on your screen at once.

### 🧩 Kanban View
The Kanban view is perfect for visualizing workflow stages.
- **Columns:** Tasks are organized into columns representing their current status (e.g., *Pending*, *In Progress*, *In Review*, *Completed*).
- **Drag & Drop:** You can easily update a task's status by clicking and dragging the card from one column to another.
- **Card Details:** Click on any card to open the modal window and view detailed information.

### 📅 Calendar View
The Calendar view provides a timeline for deadlines and major milestones.
- **Visualizing Deadlines:** See when tasks are due across a monthly, weekly, or daily view.
- **Drag & Drop:** Adjust deadlines by dragging items across the calendar dates.

> [!TIP]
> Use the sidebar toggle to switch seamlessly between these three views without losing your place!

## 3. Approval Workflow Overview

One of FACT's core features is the **Dynamic Approval Workflow**, which automates how documents are routed for signature and approval.

**How it works:**
1. A document or task is submitted via the Intake Form.
2. An administrator or designated reviewer reviews the pending intake and initiates the workflow.
3. **Approvers** receive an automated email notification when it is their turn to act.

**Acting as an Approver:**
If you are an assigned approver on an active step, you can take action in two ways:
- **Directly from Email:** Click the "Approve" or "Decline" buttons embedded in the notification email you receive.
- **From the Web App:** Open the **Approval Queue Page** from the navigation menu to review and approve or decline pending documents assigned to you.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All approvers must be valid `@gsa.gov` email addresses. The system verifies this securely via your Google Workspace login.

## 4. Your First Actions

To get comfortable with the system, try these first steps:

### 1. Create a New Task
Click the **"+ New Action Item"** or **"Intake Form"** button, fill out the required details (Title, Assignee, Deadline), and click submit to add a new item to the backlog.

### 2. Update a Status
Switch to the **Kanban View** and drag your new task from the "Pending" column to the "In Progress" column.

### 3. Add a Note
Open the detailed modal window for your task (by clicking its link in the Grid view or clicking the card in the Kanban view) and navigate to the notes or comments section to leave an update.

### 4. Route a Document for Approval
While viewing a task, navigate to the **Approval** tab. Here you can attach the document you wish to have reviewed and initialize the routing workflow.
