# FACT System: Executive Cheat Sheet

Welcome to FACT! This 1-page guide is designed for Executives and Executive Officers. It covers the core workflows you need to manage your portfolio, track health, and approve documents in 60 seconds or less.

## 1. Accessing Your Dashboard

- **Login URL:** (Insert your Hub or Sandbox URL here)
- **First Look:** When you log in, you will land on the **Morning Briefing / Pulse Check**.
- **What it does:** This view instantly highlights items that are "Action Needed", overdue, or blocked.

## 2. The 30-Second Daily Check

You don't need to dig through spreadsheets. Just check these three areas:

1. **Portfolio Health (Pulse Tab):** Look for the red "Action Needed" or yellow "At Risk" badges. These are the items your team needs help unblocking.
2. **The Intake Queue (📥 Intake):** If your team uses email-to-task routing, this is where new requests land. Ensure someone on your team has claimed or reviewed them.
3. **The Approval Queue (✅ Approvals):** This is your most important tab. Any document or decision that requires your sign-off will appear here.

## 3. Approving & Declining Documents

When a document requires your approval, you have two ways to handle it:

### Method A: Approve via the Web App (Recommended)
1. Click the **✅ Approvals** tab in the main navigation.
2. Locate the item pending your review. 
3. Click the **Drive Link** to open the document and review it.
4. Click the green **Approve** button or the red **Decline** button.
5. (Optional) Add a comment in the popup to explain your decision or request changes.

### Method B: Approve via Email
When a workflow step is assigned to you, you will receive an automated email from the system.
1. Open the email. It contains a direct link to the document and a summary of the request.
2. Click the **Approve** or **Decline** link directly inside the email body.
3. A browser tab will open confirming your decision has been recorded.

> [!TIP]
> If you decline a document, the system automatically routes it back to the original author with a notification so they can fix it and resubmit!

## 4. Searching and Filtering

Need to find a specific data call or project?
- Use the **Search Bar** at the top right of the application to search by Title, Owner, or ID.
- Switch to the **Grid View (table icon)** and use the column headers to sort by Due Date or Status.
